# Remaining visual issues (after v1)

Checked on the Rice/HLE plugin in mupen64plus. Not yet checked on real hardware or in RetroArch (ParaLLEl).

1. **Body is still Mario's build.** The torso, butt and thighs are wide and round. Haylie's slimmer proportions need the torso, legs and arms rescaled per bone, with seam checks at the shoulders and hips.
2. **Glasses temples float.** Behind the lenses, the arms are thin bars that cross the cap's side panel. They should tuck under the cap and end at a modelled ear.
3. **Smile and eyes are always on.** The face is fixed and happy. The "dead" and hurt eyes are still Mario's X-eyes texture.
4. **Ponytail is rigid.** It follows the head bone only, so it doesn't swing during runs or flips. It may poke through the back while swimming, crawling or on a pole. Only idle, run, jump, triple jump, crouch and punch were screenshot-tested.
5. **Cap shape is Mario's cap.** The concept shows a rounder baseball cap with a longer brim.
6. **Hair under the cap is minimal.** It's just the brown locks and the back hair. There are no bangs, and the cap-off hair is Mario's haircut recolored brown.
7. **Hands-holding-cap and wing-cap hand models** use the heart logo, but their cap shape is still Mario's.
8. **Low-poly (distant) head** gets the glasses and ponytail but keeps its own simpler face.
9. **Mario identity elsewhere**:
   - Title screen (Goddard Mario head and logo)
   - "MARIO A–D" save names and file-select icons
   - Dialog text and the castle painting / Peach's window
   - Voice clips ("It's-a me")
   - Credits, the ending, and the "Mario" text in the pause menu
10. **HUD icon is 16×16 pixel art.** The glasses are faint at that size.
11. **Metal Haylie**: the glasses, ponytail, smile and bib heart pick up the chrome effect correctly. The tongue isn't drawn in Metal mode, which is intentional.
